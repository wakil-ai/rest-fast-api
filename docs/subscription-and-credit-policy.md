# Subscription and credit policy (draft)

This product policy belongs in WakilAI's Terms of Use, not the Privacy Policy.
Publish it on the legal site and link that public Terms page from the mobile apps
before treating it as customer-facing legal text.

## Plans and credits

- Standard includes 6,000 credits per billing period.
- Pro includes 18,000 credits per billing period.
- Subscription credits expire when the paid billing period ends. They do not
  roll into a later period and are not a separate top-up balance.
- If WakilAI offers one-time credit packs later, they must be identified as a
  separate balance with their own price and expiry terms.

## Standard to Pro

- An active Standard customer may upgrade to Pro for the same billing period.
- Pro access begins immediately. The current billing end date does not become
  an additional paid month.
- The current-period allowance becomes Pro's 18,000-credit allowance. Credits
  already used during that period count against that allowance; Standard and
  Pro allowances are never added together.
- For direct local payments, WakilAI credits the unused value of Standard toward
  the Pro price. Apple App Store and Google Play apply their own store-approved
  proration and replacement rules.

## Cancellation and plan changes

- Customers cannot downgrade from Pro to Standard during an active paid period.
- Cancelling turns off future renewal only. Access remains available through
  the displayed billing end date, then the account returns to the free plan.
- Refunds follow the applicable store policy and mandatory consumer-protection
  rules; a cancellation alone does not shorten an already-paid billing period.
